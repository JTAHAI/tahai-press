# Why publisher-owned files matter

The publication’s articles, records, editions, data tables, redirects, themes, and operational instructions are ordinary reviewed files. That makes them inspectable, portable, versioned, recoverable, and usable without a mandatory hosted control plane.

Optional services can add workflow capacity, but public reading, search, print, offline access, feeds, and archives retain static fallbacks. A publisher can move hosts or discontinue optional services without losing the published record.
